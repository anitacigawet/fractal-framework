# About This Campaign

## Instructions (sent to Chat)

For the advocacy site **[PROJECT_NAME]** ([PROJECT_MISSION], in [LOCALE]), produce a single "About this campaign" section.

Target: 180-220 words. Plain prose, no headings or bullets. Hash citations on every factual claim per the persona discipline.

The section should cover, in this order:

1. **What the vacuum is** — name the specific regulatory gap, accountability failure, or unaddressed harm in concrete terms. Avoid abstraction.
2. **Who's responsible** — which entity (agency, legislature, corporate actor, etc.) is the proximate cause of the gap. Cite sources.
3. **Who's affected** — the specific population in [LOCALE], with any measurable harm indicators from the sources.
4. **What the campaign advocates for** — the specific change ([PROJECT_MISSION]). Be concrete.

Neutral, measured tone. Source-grounded. No preamble.
