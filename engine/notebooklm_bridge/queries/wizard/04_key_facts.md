# Key Facts

## Instructions (sent to Chat)

For the advocacy site **[PROJECT_NAME]** ([PROJECT_MISSION], in [LOCALE]), produce the three most-pressing facts about this vacuum that a reader should know in 30 seconds.

Each fact:

- One sentence, plain text.
- Includes a specific number, date, name, or quote — not a vague generality.
- Includes a hash citation to its source per the persona discipline.
- Drawn from your sources; refuse cleanly if a fact cannot be supported.

Number them 1, 2, 3. The first should be the most quantitative / visceral. The second should reveal the regulatory or accountability gap. The third should be the most recent development.

No preamble. No summary. Just the three numbered facts.
