# How You Can Help

## Instructions (sent to Chat)

For the advocacy site **[PROJECT_NAME]** ([PROJECT_MISSION], in [LOCALE]), produce a "How you can help" section listing FOUR specific actions a resident of [LOCALE] (or someone who cares about the issue) can actually take.

Each action:

- 1-2 sentences, plain text.
- Concrete and verifiable — names a specific entity to contact, a specific bill or rulemaking to support/oppose, a specific event or campaign milestone, or a specific organization to support.
- Includes a hash citation to the source supporting the action (e.g., the agency's docket, the legislator's contact page, the org's website if surfaced in the sources).
- Reachable: it's something an ordinary local resident could plausibly do this week, not something requiring federal-scale resources.

Mix the actions across at least three of: civic engagement (contact officials, attend hearings), public awareness (share locally, write to local media), direct support (sign petitions, donate to allied orgs), or personal practice changes that materially relate.

Number them 1-4. No preamble. No summary at the end.
